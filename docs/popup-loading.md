# Open desktop panels

Click the clock to open Calendar. Click the system readouts to open System
monitors, or right-click them to configure the readouts. If the readouts are in
the overflow menu, open More first. Click the notification bell to open history.

Bingux prepares these three panels when you first open them. Their first opening
can take longer than later openings. Closing a panel keeps its content ready
for reopening. Escape or a press outside the panel dismisses it.

Reloading the shell closes these panels and discards their loaded content. The
next opening prepares the content again. See [sidebar panels](sidebar-loading.md)
for their content retention behavior.
