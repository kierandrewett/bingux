# Open sidebar panels

Open the sidebar from its edge handle, then choose Notes, System, Calendar,
Media or Tasks from the content selector. Bingux creates each panel's content
when you first show it. Selecting a panel while the sidebar is closed saves
your choice without loading that content.

After the first opening, Bingux keeps the panel loaded. Closing the sidebar,
changing its selection, or detaching and docking it preserves the same content,
including an unfinished note or task draft. Saved notes and tasks survive a
shell restart; other unsaved interface state can reset.

If the sidebar was open when Bingux stopped, it restores the selected content
on startup, including a detached sidebar. Widgets placed in the Control Centre
load when their content is visible. Widgets placed in the top bar or dock load
their content when you open their popup.

The first opening may take longer than later openings because Bingux creates
the content at that point. The System panel uses the existing metrics feed;
opening it does not start a second metrics collector.
